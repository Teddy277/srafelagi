Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Srafelagi aggregates job listings from public sources (EthioJobs, GeezJobs, Afriwork, Telegram channels, and others). This policy explains how we handle information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Saved jobs: If you use “Save” on a job, we store the list of saved job IDs in your browser only (localStorage). We do not send this to our server or store it in our database.
  • Usage: Our server may log standard access data (e.g. IP, time, requested URL) for security and operation. We do not use this to identify you personally.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or signup to browse or search jobs.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not track you across other sites for advertising.

Job listings and external sites

Job details and application links point to external websites (employers, job boards, Telegram). Those sites have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for how they collect or use your data when you apply or visit them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at the email or link provided in the footer of the main site.
