Amen Manufacturing PLC External Vacancy  Announcement

Job Position -1 Junior Export Coffee Laboratory Technician

Required  Qualifications and Experience
Education:BSc/Diploma in Food Science, Chemistry, Biology, Agriculture (with a focus on coffee/horticulture) or a related field.
Certified in the field from nationally accredited institutions and A minimum of 1 year of practical work experience in a laboratory setting, preferably within the coffee, food processing, or beverage industry.
Basic understanding of green coffee quality parameters and defects is a strong advantage.
Ability to perform precise, repetitive tasks with a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail.
Good numerical and record-keeping skills.
Proficient in basic computer applications (MS Office).
Strong communication skills in Amharic; working knowledge of English is required.
A team player with a willingness to learn and a strong work ethic.

Requirement Skill
Strong communication skills in Amharic; working knowledge of English is required.

Job Position -2 Storekeeper

Required  Qualifications and Experience
Education:Diploma or above in Supplies Management, Logistics, Business Administration, Accounting or a related field.
A minimum of 1 year of proven work experience as a Storekeeper, Warehouse Assistant, or in a similar inventory management role.
Experience in an import-export or fast-moving trading company is a distinct advantage.
Strong organizational and record-keeping skills with exceptional attention to detail.
Basic proficiency in computer applications (MS Office, especially Excel). Experience with inventory management software is a plus.
Physical fitness and ability to handle manual tasks, including lifting and moving items.
High level of integrity, reliability, and responsibility.
Good communication skills in Afan Oromo, Amharic; working knowledge of English is beneficial.
Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Requirement Skill
Strong communication skills in Amharic; working knowledge of English is required.

Location: Addis Ababa
Deadline : February 3rd, 2026

How To Apply
Interested and qualified candidates are invited to submit their CV and a cover letter via email to [email protected]
or [email protected]
Please indicate “Application for Junior Export Coffee Lab Technician” in the subject line of your email.
Only short-listed candidates will be contacted for an interview.
